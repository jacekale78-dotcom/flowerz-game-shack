COVER IMAGE CREDITS
All images in this folder are real screenshots captured by Flowerz Game Shack from the game
pages that are actually bundled in this site. None are AI-generated, and none are copied from
another website or publisher.

- 2048, pacman, tetris, clumsy-bird, asteroids, chrome-dino, breakout, snake, space-invaders,
  hextris, particle-clicker, chess, tower-blocks, skifree, solitaire:
  screenshots of Flowerz's own prototype pages in /games (original Flowerz code).
- hexgl.webp: screenshot of the locally hosted HexGL build (games/hexgl), MIT License,
  (c) 2015 Thibaut Despoulain (BKcore). https://github.com/BKcore/HexGL

Games with no entry here (Cookie Clicker, Run 3, Retro Bowl, Basketball Stars, Subway Surfers,
Drive Mad, Stickman Hook, Moto X3M, Slope, Trigger Rally, and the three collections) use the
purple placeholder card art, because their official art/screenshots are copyrighted and could
not be sourced with clear reuse permission. To add one, drop a 480x293 WebP in /covers and
set "cover":"covers/<id>.webp" on that game in index.html.

- kick-the-buddy.webp: screenshot of the locally hosted Kick the Buddy build (games/kick-the-buddy),
  by ForrestDevs. Captured by Flowerz Game Shack.
